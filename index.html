BEFORE YOU HUNT

Wildlife comes first.

No photograph is worth stressing or endangering an animal.

Keep a respectful distance from birds and other wildlife.
Never disturb nests, chicks or breeding animals.
Never chase, flush, bait, handle or corner wildlife for a challenge.
Stay out of restricted areas and respect private property.
If an animal changes its behaviour because of you, move back or leave.
THE SIMPLE RULES
• Keep five cards in your hand.
• One photograph claims one challenge card.
• The photo must genuinely match the challenge.
• In Social, the other player votes on your claim.
• Accepted claims earn the Wing Points printed on the card.
• Never put wildlife, yourself or other people at risk for a card.
SHUTTERWING rule: Missing the shot is always better than disturbing the wildlife.
